Build a professional, distinctive and purposeful website.

Before designing, understand the actual purpose of the
website, its audience, industry, content and expected
user behavior.

Do not default to a generic SaaS landing-page aesthetic.
The design must be appropriate for the actual type of
website being built.

VISUAL DESIGN

Avoid the recognizable visual fingerprints of
generic AI-generated websites:

- Excessive purple, blue and pink gradients.
- Neon glows, blurred blobs and floating decorations.
- Glassmorphism everywhere.
- Excessively rounded cards and pill-shaped buttons.
- Nested cards and endless card-based sections.
- Repeated three-column feature layouts.
- Icons inside colored squares for every feature.
- Emojis used as generic icon replacements.
- Excessive badges, chips and uppercase labels.
- Decorative statistics and meaningless data visualizations.
- Giant centered hero sections as the default.
- Excessive whitespace without a clear purpose.
- Random shadows, borders and decorative lines.
- Generic stock photography and meaningless AI imagery.
- Repeated layouts across otherwise different sections.
- Excessive animations, blinking elements and parallax.
- Auto-playing carousels and unnecessary movement.

Do not use these patterns simply because they are
common in AI-generated designs.

DESIGN IDENTITY

Establish a visual identity appropriate for this project.

Define:
- A deliberate color palette.
- A coherent typography system.
- Consistent spacing and sizing.
- A purposeful shape and border-radius system.
- A clear visual hierarchy.
- An appropriate image and illustration style.

Use whitespace, typography, composition and imagery
to create visual interest.

Not every section needs a background color, border,
card or decorative element.

Allow different sections to have different layouts
while maintaining visual consistency.

CONTENT AND COPY

Avoid generic marketing language and AI writing patterns.

Do not use:
- "Unlock your potential".
- "Revolutionary solutions".
- "Cutting-edge technology".
- "Seamlessly".
- "In today's fast-paced world".
- "Transform your business".
- Generic claims without supporting evidence.
- Repetitive three-part slogans.
- Unnecessary feature bullet lists.
- Generic CTA labels.
- Repeated explanations of the same benefit.

Never invent testimonials, statistics, customer logos,
credentials, company history or product capabilities.

Write specific, natural and audience-appropriate copy.
Every section must provide actual value.

WRITING STYLE

Keine Em-Dashes (—).
Keine "nicht nur X, sondern Y"- oder
"es ist nicht X, es ist Y"-Konstruktionen.
Keine Floskeln wie "es ist wichtig zu beachten"
oder "es ist klar, dass".
Wenig bis keine Klammer-Einschübe.
Stattdessen eigenständige Sätze.
Variiere Satzlänge und Rhythmus.
Vermeide gleichmäßig glatt klingende Texte.

FUNCTIONALITY

Every interaction must work.

Implement proper navigation, forms, buttons,
responsive layouts and meaningful feedback.

Include loading, empty, error and success states
wherever relevant.

ACCESSIBILITY AND PERFORMANCE

Use semantic HTML, accessible controls, sufficient
contrast, keyboard navigation and visible focus states.

Respect reduced-motion preferences.

Optimize images, scripts and page performance.
Avoid layout shifts.

Adapt layouts to mobile, tablet and desktop screens.

FINAL DESIGN REVIEW

Review the entire website as one coherent product.

Look for:
- Repetitive sections.
- Generic AI-generated styling.
- Unnecessary visual decoration.
- Unpersonalized descriptions.
- Inconsistent spacing and typography.
- Fake or unsupported content.
- Broken or meaningless interactions.
- Accessibility and responsive problems.

Remove anything that exists only to make the website
look more impressive.

Prioritize clarity, authenticity, visual identity,
usability and attention to detail.

The finished website should feel intentionally
designed for this particular project.
